Picking a model on OpenCode Go means solving a billing puzzle, not an AI one. Tariffs come with quota multipliers that quietly triple the price, every session splits into input / cache-read / cache-write / output, and a tariff named “Flash” can cost four times what its “Max” sibling does — with nothing explaining how that relates to how smart the model actually is. Good luck comparing anything by eyeballing a price list. This page pairs each tariff with an independent benchmark score and works out the honest number: what one average task costs in dollars, how many millipercents of your monthly plan it eats, and how much intelligence you get per unit. No marketing, no “premium” labels — just price against measured capability.
